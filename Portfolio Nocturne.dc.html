Case study 01
ME 1212 · LSUTeam of 6

Reverse Engineering — Electric Tire Pump

A complete teardown of an electromechanical tire pump: how it moves air, where it wastes effort, and how I'd redesign it.

Process
  1. Disassembled the pump and catalogued every mechanical component.
  2. Evaluated mechanical components, airflow paths, and system performance.
  3. Modeled internal components in 3D CAD and produced AutoCAD drawings.
  4. Compiled findings into a structured engineering technical report.
Findings
  • Identified design limitations in the pump's mechanical layout.
  • Proposed design improvements targeting performance and serviceability.
Lessons
  • Form/fit/function analysis on real hardware.
  • Translating measured parts into accurate CAD.
  • Writing to an engineering-report standard.

Case study 02
CM 1020 · LSUTeam of 2

3D Printer Component Analysis

A component-level technical brief on a disassembled 3D printer — what each part does, how it's made, and why those materials.

Process
  1. Disassembled the printer and identified fasteners, joints, springs, bearings, gears, pulleys, and belts.
  2. Studied the manufacturing processes and material selection behind each component.
  3. Documented component relationships and mechanism behavior in a structured brief.
Findings
  • Mapped component relationships into a full mechanism description.
  • Connected each material choice to component function and manufacturing method.
Lessons
  • Fast, accurate component identification.
  • Reading a machine as a system, not a parts list.
Mechanical systems analysisComponent identificationManufacturing knowledge
Case study 03
CM 1020 · LSUTeam of 2

Mechanical Clamp — CAD Design Package

A production-ready CAD package for a mechanical clamp assembly: every part modeled, dimensioned, costed, and documented.

Process
  1. Modeled the complete clamp assembly in SolidWorks.
  2. Produced detailed dimensioned drawings for every component.
  3. Built the bill of materials and assembly documentation.
  4. Analyzed manufacturing methods and material costs.
  5. Generated exploded views and component specifications.
Findings
  • Delivered a complete drawing package with GD&T to ANSI standards.
  • Costed materials and manufacturing methods per component.
Lessons
  • GD&T applied under real drawing standards.
  • Documentation is the deliverable — not an afterthought.

Case study 04
ME 3633 · LSUTeam of 6

Manufacturing Processes — Casting & Fabrication in Aircraft Engines

A research study of how aircraft-engine components are actually made — where casting wins, where fabrication wins, and why.

Process
  1. Researched machining technologies used in aircraft-engine production.
  2. Identified which engine components rely on casting versus fabrication.
  3. Weighed the advantages and disadvantages of each technique.
  4. Illustrated the report with models and figures of real examples.
Findings
  • Matched component families to their production methods.
  • Documented the trade-offs that drive casting vs. fabrication decisions.
Lessons
  • Manufacturing-process literacy that transfers directly to automotive powertrains and structures.
